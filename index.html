Cyto-Histopathological Correlation of Abnormal Cervical Screening at UNIMAS

Principal Student Investigator • Academic Session 2023/2024

Translating Clinical Medical Research into an Industry-Standard Case Study

For my Capstone Project, I reframed my UNIMAS undergraduate medical research study (conducted during the 2023/2024 academic session) into an employer-ready operational case study structured around the five-pillar Industry Case Study Mindset (Context, Problem, Constraints, Process, and Impact). Executed in collaboration with the Departments of Pathology and Obstetrics & Gynaecology, this seven-year STARD-compliant audit (2017–2023) evaluated 238 consecutive referrals at the Women Health and Infertility Clinic, UNIMAS, establishing Sarawak's first local diagnostic accuracy benchmarks and an actionable four-rule risk-stratified colposcopy decision protocol.

STARD Diagnostic Audit IBM SPSS v28.0 Biostatistics 5-Whys Root Cause Analysis UNIMAS Ethics Clearance (FME/24/03)

Capstone: Written Portfolio

Comprehensive Case Study Report, Synoptic Proforma & Ethics Clearance

Written Deliverable • Full Report

Capstone Project Case Study Report (20 Pages)

Download PDF

Comprehensive ten-chapter case study report detailing the executive summary, project context (Sarawak's state-leading cervical cancer incidence of 12.1 per 100,000 women vs 6.0 nationally), problem re-definition (unquantified cyto-histopathological discordance and a 9.6% non-diagnostic biopsy rate in women aged ≥50 years due to Type 3 Transformation Zones), STARD disposition of 230 eligible patients, four-stage governance framework, and the four-rule risk-stratified colposcopy protocol. Includes Appendix A (Revised Synoptic IFCPC/LAST Proforma) and Appendix B (UNIMAS Medical Ethics Committee Approval FME/24/03).

Key Diagnostic Finding:

Combined co-testing (HPV-16/18 or high-grade cytology) achieved 100.0% sensitivity and 100.0% NPV for detecting high-grade CIN II+ lesions.

Root Cause & Clinical Fix:

68.8% (11/16) of insufficient biopsies clustered in women aged ≥50 years; mandating concurrent Endocervical Curettage (ECC) resolves this sampling gap.

Resource Optimisation:

Deferring biopsy in normal Swede Score < 5 low-grade referrals safely spares 27.8% of patients unnecessary invasive procedures.
